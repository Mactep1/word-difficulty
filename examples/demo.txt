The abandoned observatory

Alice discovered an abandoned observatory near the village. The instruments were delicate, but the machinery was remarkably resilient.

She investigated the mechanism. The investigators had investigated similar mechanisms before. Their investigation revealed an astonishing discrepancy between the measurements and the original hypothesis.

The children read their books at home. They were happy to help. Meanwhile, a luminous zephyrium glimmered beneath the telescope.

The committee decided to abolish the obsolete regulation. Its decision would fundamentally transform the institution.
